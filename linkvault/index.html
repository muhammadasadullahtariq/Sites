Good questions.

01Is Link Vault the same as Link App?

Yes. Link Vault is the next version of Link App from the same team, with faster sync, stronger bookmark security and a redesigned experience.

02Is Link Vault free?

Yes. Link Vault is free to download on iPhone, iPad and Android, and the browser extension is free. An optional premium upgrade unlocks extra features.

03How does Link Vault decide which folder a link goes in?

Link Vault recognizes what kind of link you saved and files it with similar links, so recipes go with recipes and videos go to Watch Later. You can move anything, and create your own folders with custom icons and colors.

04Which apps can I save links from?

Any app with a Share button, including Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X, Reddit, Safari, Chrome and shopping apps. On a computer, use the extension for Chrome, Safari or Brave.

05Can I keep some links private?

Yes. Lock any folder with Face ID or a passcode, or hide it so it doesn't appear until you unlock it.

06Which devices does it work on?

iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android and your web browser. Your links sync between them and stay available offline.

07How do I download Link Vault?

On your phone, tap Download the app on this page and you'll go straight to the App Store or Google Play. On a computer, scan the QR code with your phone's camera.
